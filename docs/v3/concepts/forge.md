# Ember Bell — a FORGE treatment

**Logline:** In a foundry at the bottom of the night, one pixel spark is hammered, poured, buried and cast into a bell of light, and on the last G the bell rings the darkness into dawn.

## 1. World and rules

- **Darkness is cold metal**, soot-purple (#06030a/#140820). There are no lamps: everything glows only by its own heat.
- **Light is heat.** One ramp sets every colour: #3b0f4f → #7a2fa0 → #b3162e → #e0352b → #ff7b1c → #ffae2b → #ffd84a → #fff3c4.
- **Heat spreads by striking.** A strike's heat conducts outward along the geometry as a band, then cools toward purple.
- **The heat floor rises scene by scene.** The dark never returns as deep as at 0 s.

## 2. Cast

**Protagonist: the Spark (M1).** A pixel diamond with a one-pixel cross flare, drawn on the motif layer. Each M1 note is a **hop**, with pitch setting the height. In the intro it hops staccato and rests in the arp's gaps. In the finale it hops legato, trailing a ribbon, 1.5× larger. It is the bell's seed and ends as its **clapper**.

| Voice | Manifestation |
|---|---|
| Kick | **Hammer.** A dark slab falls from off-frame onto the anvil. The syncopated 16ths 7–8 are the smith's double-tap bounce. |
| Snare | **Bellows.** The coal bed puffs brighter on 2 and 4. The fills are rapid pumping. |
| Hats | **Sparks.** Ballistic embers that live under 0.4 s. |
| Bass | **The molten mass.** It sets the heat of the crucible and coal bed: amber on the D pedal, crimson or darker without bass. |
| Pads | **Smoke veils**, only in the pauses. |
| Vox texture | **The bell's ghost.** Rings expand from where the bell *will* hang: a stab makes one ring, a swell a wide one. |
| M2 | **The crucible tips:** C tilts it, D pours. Bar 21 (on B) is a crimson pour. |
| M4 | **The seal.** A glowing seam closes into a circle: one lap done. |
| M6 | **The furnace door.** A vertical slit of light opens and pulses on the 8 hammered Ds: something is about to change. |
| M5 | **Chimney flare**, a spark column stepping up. |

## 4. Callbacks

1. **The breath (8.75 / 18.05 / 63.62).** The same frozen pose each time. At 18.05 only the Spark stays lit. At 63.62 the Spark is inside the bell, so the release flashes flare-white, not orange.
2. **A at 0 s ↔ A at 54.9 s.** Identical camera and hop path. The anvil is warm now (Em7 → G), the bell hangs above as a mirror, and the ghost ring has a source.
3. **B ↔ finale B.** The same strikes on the same kicks, now on the bell. Dots become a ribbon, and light rises from 0.5 to 0.8.
4. **Runners → landscape.** The channels filled in C become the lit ground in B′.
5. **M4 ring.** A bent bar (16.04), then a hoist halo (70.9), then the final halo (80.04).
6. **M6 door.** It opens onto darkness at 34.32, and wider onto the humming bell at 52.61.
7. **B7 shadow** (29.75 / 41.18 / 48.04). A purple crust each time, and never in the finale.

## 6. Signature moments

1. **First Strike (9.18).** After 0.42 s of frozen dark, the hammer flattens the Spark: a white-orange flash, a radial spark fan, and the whole coal bed lights as the low band jumps from 10 % to 56 %.
2. **The Bell Sings Alone (60.24).** The untouched bell rings once: a perfect circle of light crosses the frame, and the Spark stops hopping.
3. **Dawn Toll (84.62).** With every spark dead, the Spark strikes once from inside, and a flare floods the frame, trembling with the chord.

## 7. Feasibility

**Six components plus the motif layer:**

| Component | Scenes it plays |
|---|---|
| `AnvilDark` | anvil-dark, anvil-memory |
| `ForgeStrike` | forge-strike, bell-strike |
| `PourRunners` | pour-runners |
| `MouldCooling` | mould-cooling |
| `LatheFinish` | lathe-finish |
| `SkyRing` | sky-ring, dawn-toll |

**How each element is built:**

- **Bell.** `LatheGeometry` (12-point profile) with an emissive `heat(y, t)` shader. Bands start on kick frames and decay analytically. The reflection is a fake specular blob at the Spark's projected position.
- **Anvil and hammer.** An extruded silhouette and a box on an eased curve. **The smith is never shown**, so there is no figure animation.
- **Runners and mould.** Plane shaders: a ring-and-spoke SDF whose `fill` advances on each M2, and Voronoi cracks.
- **Rings, smoke, sky.** Additive annulus shaders, noise quads, and a gradient dome with a `temperature` uniform.
- **Hard parts, faked.**
  - Heat diffusion: distance-from-strike × time-since-strike.
  - The pour: a `TubeGeometry` with scrolling noise.
  - The swing: an analytic pendulum.
- **Determinism.** Particles are closed-form from (emit frame, seed).
