# Upwelling

**Logline:** A spark of plankton, born in the lightless deep, rides the nightly rise of the glowing sea creatures, is left behind when they turn back at dawn, and is carried by a whale to the surface in time to see its first sunrise. There its own glow disappears into the daylight.

## 1. World and rules

- **Darkness is depth.** The frame is a water column. Deep water is #06030a/#140820, mid-water #3b0f4f→#7a2fa0, and near the surface it turns crimson, then red. The sky above is orange→amber→flare. The camera's height *is* the light level, so the arc is a literal ascent.
- **Light is bioluminescence.** Creatures glow orange/amber; the protagonist glows yellow/flare. Light spreads by **contagion**: one flash triggers its neighbours. The opposite is defensive blackout: everyone goes dark at once.
- **Direction:** in the deep, marine snow falls. Near the surface, bubbles rise.
- **The twist:** real plankton rise at night and *sink at dawn*. The shoal obeys that rule. The spark breaks it.

## 4. Callbacks

- **Held breath (8.746 / 63.62):** both times the particles freeze and the Spark shrinks to one pixel. First it is snow falling into black; then bubbles rising into amber. Inside the second gap, the faint −44 dB note becomes **one blink**: the Spark is no longer afraid.
- **Intro A (0.00 / 54.90):** same lens, position and flicker. The darkness above becomes a caustic net, and with Em7→G the net settles from jitter into a stable pattern. The distant snares are the shoal remembering the Spark.
- **First parade B (9.18 / 64.04):** both open with a glyph iris, and the creature formation and kick choreography are identical. The second time, the surface splits the frame and the parade has a sky.
- **Gyre M4 ×3:** it closes into blackout (16.04), launches the spout (70.90), and becomes the sun's halo (80.04 → 84.62).
- **Whale song:** the stab rings are distant. Only the 60.24 ring crosses the whole frame.
- **M6 ×2:** the first turns the shoal away. The second makes the whale let go.
- **Shadow → saviour:** the eclipse at 29.75 is the silhouette that rises under the Spark at 45.75.

## 6. Signature moments

1. **The cascade (9.18, f551).** After 0.42 s of stillness, a single pixel opens like an eye. A glyph-shaped iris blooms and hundreds of creatures ignite, pulsing upward on the first kick.
2. **The dark below moves (45.5, f2730).** In the loneliest shot the Spark climbs alone, and the black beneath it turns out to be alive. The crimson-rimmed whale we feared at 29.75 rises and lifts it. At 60.24 its song ring climbs the frame, and the Spark answers.
3. **First light (84.62, f5077).** A tiny yellow pixel floats beside the rising sun, and their reflection columns merge. On the release it dims: in daylight its glow is no longer needed.

## 7. Feasibility

- **Whale (hardest):** never shown whole. Build it as a `LatheGeometry` body plus flattened-ellipsoid fins with a vertex-shader flex: near-black, crimson fresnel rim, a row of faint glowing spots. It is framed as a back or silhouette. Fallback: a fullscreen SDF capsule.
- **Creatures:** jellies are instanced half-spheres plus line tentacles (about 40). Chains are beads on a CatmullRom curve with a sweeping light index. The fish school is about 1000 instanced diamonds on curl-noise flow. Everything is seeded and frame-deterministic.
- **Caustics:** a Voronoi fragment shader on a plane.
- **Surface split:** no refraction. Two gradients, a wavy SDF meniscus, additive sun streaks and glints. Keep the waterline at y = 0.55 so it reads at 1080².
- **Snow, bubbles and spout:** the existing ember system (drift and scatter).
- **Components (5):** `DeepWater` (abyss, alone, under-glass), `Column` (ascent, shoal, thermocline), `WhaleRide`, `Surface` (breach, spray), `Horizon`. The shared actors are Whale, Jelly, Chain, FishSchool and Caustics. That gives **10 timeline scenes from 5 components.**
